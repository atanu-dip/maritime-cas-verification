
1. Perception costs a large, measurable fraction of the safety that the
   perfect-information harness reported.
   Mean safety: 0.825 (truth) -> 0.537 (perception), a change of -0.288.
   Collisions: 0.041 -> 0.097.
   Hardest hit: MPC (-0.427) -- the strategy
   that optimises hardest against the state estimate loses the most when
   that estimate is wrong.

2. Geometry, not sensor quality, sets the range limit.
   Horizon at 8.6 nm; beyond it a vessel is hull-down
   and the waterline range cue does not exist at all. Range uncertainty grows
   as R^2 for both monocular cues, which is projection geometry rather than a
   property of the detector.

3. Velocity is not observable from one frame, and the delay is expensive.
   Mean time to a usable track: 19.8 min.
   Mean TCPA remaining at that moment: 4.0 min.
   COLREGS Rule 8(a) requires avoiding action 'in ample time'. The filter
   spends much of that time before it can say anything about course and speed.

4. COLREGS role inversion is real in this phase, and its cause is POSITION
   ERROR rather than misclassification.
   Mean role-flip rate: 0.235.
   Measured Rule 18 class confusion: 0.000.
   SMD annotates craft type, not legal status: only 'Sail boat' maps to a
   privileged COLREGS category, and the detector never confuses it. The
   by-status split confirms the mechanism -- inversions occur only for
   power-driven targets, because Rule 18 pins the role for privileged
   vessels no matter where they appear to be. Classification-driven
   inversion is deferred to Phase 2b, where CLIP supplies the category
   zero-shot for the classes no maritime dataset labels.

5. Camera resolution: measured, not assumed.
      640×360: safety 0.524, detection 0.024, track delay 21.0 min
     1280×720: safety 0.548, detection 0.042, track delay 19.5 min
    1920×1080: safety 0.614, detection 0.059, track delay 18.9 min
    3840×2160: safety 0.676, detection 0.087, track delay 16.6 min
   Span 0.152, monotonic in resolution: resolution is a material constraint.

6. COLREGS compliance ROSE under perception (max +0.177, Velocity obstacle).
   This is an artefact and must not be read as an improvement: a CAS with an
   unusable track manoeuvres less, and several rules -- notably Rule 17's
   'keep your course and speed' -- reward inaction. Safety and compliance
   have to be read together.


Methodological note:
  scenario sampler : scenic
  detector         : YOLOv8
  error model      : fitted from 4455 instances on real imagery, content scaled at fixed network input (apparent height 1.8-643 px)
  horizon accuracy : not validated; nominal geometric horizon used (optimistic)
  Detection performance is measured on real imagery across five scales, so
  the fitted curve covers the apparent sizes the encounters actually
  produce. The closed-loop campaign then runs on that fitted model, because
  no recorded video can show the view produced by a manoeuvre that never
  happened.
